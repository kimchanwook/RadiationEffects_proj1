\title{Device Degradation}
\begin{document}
\maketitle

Radiation-induced defects can degrade device behavior by increasing leakage current, lowering charge collection efficiency, and reducing diffusion length.

A simple leakage-current relation is
\[
\Delta I = \alpha \Phi V,
\]
where $\alpha$ is a damage coefficient, $\Phi$ is fluence, and $V$ is the active volume.
