\section{Diffusion Length Model}
The carrier diffusion length is related to the diffusivity $D$ and effective lifetime $\teff$ by
\begin{equation}
L = \sqrt{D\,\teff}.
\end{equation}

Substituting the reduced lifetime model gives
\begin{equation}
L(\PhiF) = \sqrt{\frac{D}{\tfrac{1}{\tzero}+\beta \PhiF}}.
\end{equation}

This equation makes the qualitative physics transparent: as fluence increases, the lifetime decreases, and the diffusion length decreases accordingly. In devices that depend on minority-carrier transport or efficient charge collection, this directly produces measurable degradation.
