\section{Leakage Current Model}
A standard reduced model for radiation-induced leakage-current increase in silicon detectors is
\begin{equation}
\Delta I = \alpha \PhiF V,
\end{equation}
where $\alpha$ is the current-related damage coefficient, $\PhiF$ is the fluence, and $V$ is the active volume.

This expression is phenomenological but very useful in practice. It condenses complex defect-assisted generation physics into a single damage parameter that scales linearly with exposure and device volume.

For presentation purposes, this model is particularly effective because it connects radiation damage to a directly measurable electrical quantity with minimal mathematical overhead.
