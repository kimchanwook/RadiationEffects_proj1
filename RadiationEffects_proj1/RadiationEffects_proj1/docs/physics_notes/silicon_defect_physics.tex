\maketitle

Defects in silicon can introduce electronic states in the bandgap. These states act as recombination and trapping centers. A simple phenomenological lifetime model is
\[
\frac{1}{\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}} = \frac{1}{\tau_0} + K_\tau N_D.
\]

As the defect density increases, carrier lifetime decreases.
