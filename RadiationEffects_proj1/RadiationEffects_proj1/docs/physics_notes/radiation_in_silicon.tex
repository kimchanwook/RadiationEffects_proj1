\title{Radiation in Silicon}
\begin{document}
\maketitle

Radiation interacting with silicon can produce both ionization and displacement damage. Ionization generates electron-hole pairs. Displacement damage occurs when enough energy is transferred to a lattice atom to knock it from its site.

For this project, the emphasis is on how transport outputs from Geant4 motivate a defect-generation model.
