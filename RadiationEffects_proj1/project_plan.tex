\section*{Objective}
Use Geant4 to model radiation transport in silicon and MATLAB to connect radiation-induced damage to semiconductor degradation and device performance loss.

\section*{Modules}
\subsection*{Module 1: Geant4}
Simulate proton or neutron transport in a silicon slab and extract deposited energy, depth-dependent deposition, and recoil-like events.

\subsection*{Module 2: Defect Model}
Use a first-order relation
\[
N_D = k_D \Phi
\]
to relate fluence $\Phi$ to defect concentration $N_D$.

\subsection*{Module 3: Lifetime Model}
Use
\[
\frac{1}{\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}} = \frac{1}{\tau_0} + K_\tau N_D
\]
to represent recombination-center driven lifetime degradation.

\subsection*{Module 4: Device Metrics}
Use diffusion length and leakage current trends:
\[
L = \sqrt{D\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}}, \qquad \Delta I = \alpha \Phi V.
\]

\section*{Deliverables}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Geant4 CSV outputs
    \item MATLAB figures for defect density, lifetime, diffusion length, and leakage current
    \item technical notes and slide outline
\end{itemize}
